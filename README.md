Say no to procrastination!

ProcrastiNO is a desktop app that helps you monitor your productivity.

How it works:
1. Tell the app what you are working on today.
2. Begin working! The app will periodicallytake a screenshot of your screen to check if you are actually working or not. The app also keeps track how to much time you spent in front of the computer vs away from the computer.
3. If you are caught procrastinating, the app will send you a notification alert to warn you.
4. When you are done working, the app gives you a breakdown of your productivity, such as how much time you spent in front of the computer, how much time you spent away from the computer, and how many times you got caught procrastinating.
